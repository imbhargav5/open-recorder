---
title: "Adjust recording audio"
description: "Route microphone channels, normalize loudness, fix audio sync, and shape tone from the Audio inspector in the video editor."
---

The **Audio** inspector in the video editor processes your recording's combined audio. Use it to fix a microphone that only plays in one speaker, even out loudness, line up audio that drifts from the video, and make voices clearer.

Every change applies to both the preview and the exported video. Slider changes are part of the editor's undo and redo history.

To open it, open a recording in the video editor and select **Audio** in the inspector.

## Voice

| Control | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Voice preset** | Applies a starting point for spoken audio: **Normalize loudness** on at −14 LUFS, **Punch** 35, **Presence** +2 dB, **Treble** +1 dB, and **Reduce low rumble** on. Your current **Channels** and **Audio Sync** settings are kept. |
| **Reset** | Restores every audio setting to its default, including **Channels** and **Audio Sync**. |
| **Channels** | Chooses how input channels reach the left and right speakers. |

**Channels** options:

| Option | Result |
| --- | --- |
| **Original stereo** | Plays the audio as recorded. This is the default. |
| **Input 1 → Mono** | Sends input 1 to both speakers. |
| **Input 2 → Mono** | Sends input 2 to both speakers. |
| **Mix both → Mono** | Averages both inputs and sends the result to both speakers. |

If your microphone is plugged into one input of a two-channel audio interface, you only hear it in one speaker. Choose the input it's connected to so you hear it in both speakers.

## Loudness

| Control | Range | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Normalize loudness** | On or off | Measures the recording's integrated loudness and adjusts the level to reach the **Target**. |
| **Target** | −24 to −9 LUFS | The loudness to normalize to. Shown only when **Normalize loudness** is on. Defaults to −14 LUFS. |
| **Gain** | −24 to +24 dB | Manual level adjustment, applied on top of normalization. |

Start with −14 LUFS and adjust from there. Open Recorder reduces gain when needed to keep samples under a −1 dB peak ceiling. This is a sample peak ceiling, not a true-peak limiter.

While Open Recorder measures the recording, the inspector shows **Measuring audio…**. When measurement finishes, it shows the current audio status.

## Audio Sync

Use **Offset** to shift the audio relative to the video by up to ±2 seconds, in 10 ms steps.

- If audio is late, move the slider left (negative).
- If audio is early, move the slider right (positive).

Click **Reset Sync** to return the offset to 0 ms.

## Tone

| Control | Range | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Punch** | 0 to 100 | Compresses louder peaks and lifts the overall level so speech sounds denser and more even. |
| **Bass** | −12 to +12 dB | Boosts or cuts low frequencies around 120 Hz. |
| **Presence** | −12 to +12 dB | Boosts or cuts upper mids around 3 kHz, where speech clarity sits. |
| **Treble** | −12 to +12 dB | Boosts or cuts high frequencies around 9 kHz. |
| **Reduce low rumble** | On or off | Filters out low-frequency noise below about 80 Hz, such as desk bumps or air conditioning hum. |

## Save audio settings in a preset

Audio settings are saved with the project. To reuse them across projects, save them along with your video appearance as a named preset from the **Project Presets** menu. See [Backgrounds](/editing/backgrounds#reusing-your-appearance-settings).
