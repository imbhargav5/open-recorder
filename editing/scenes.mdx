---
title: "3D scenes for screenshots and recordings"
description: "Tilt, rotate, and position your media in 3D with the Scene inspector, wrap it in a browser or device mockup, and animate a single motion interval on export."
---

The **Scene** inspector adds a 3D pose to the media inside the editor stage. Use it to angle a screenshot for a hero image, add depth to a recording, wrap either one in a browser or device mockup, and optionally animate one push, pull, or sweep on export. Scene is shared between the image editor and the video editor.

<Note>
Scene is labeled **Alpha** in the inspector while its geometry, mockups, and motion presets stabilize. Names, defaults, and ranges may still change.
</Note>

## When to use Scene

- Angle a screenshot before saving or copying it as a PNG.
- Wrap a screenshot or recording in a **Browser**, **Desktop Window**, **Phone**, or **Tablet** mockup.
- Add a single push-in, pull-out, tilt-reveal, or side-sweep animation to a recording or animated image export.

Scene is disabled by default: every editor opens with the pose flat, no mockup, and motion off, so nothing renders differently until you touch the inspector.

## Open the Scene inspector

1. Open a screenshot or recording in the editor.
2. In the right-side inspector rail, click **Scene** (the `rotate.3d` icon, labeled **Scene · 3D Tilt & Motion (Alpha)**).
3. Above the canvas, choose **Tilt** or **Move** to drag directly on the preview. Hold **Shift** while dragging for precision.

Click **Reset Scene** at the top of the inspector to return every Scene control to its defaults.

## Pose the media

The **Presets** section applies a starting pose in one click: **Flat**, **Tilt Left**, **Tilt Right**, or **Elevated**.

Fine-tune the pose in the **Transform** section:

| Control | Range | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Horizontal Tilt** | −60° to 60° | Rotates the media around its vertical axis. |
| **Vertical Tilt** | −60° to 60° | Rotates the media around its horizontal axis. |
| **Rotation** | −180° to 180° | Rolls the media in the plane of the canvas. |
| **Scale** | 0.25× to 2× | Shrinks or enlarges the tilted surface. |
| **Position X** | −1 to 1 | Slides the surface horizontally, in canvas widths. |
| **Position Y** | −1 to 1 | Slides the surface vertically, in canvas heights. |
| **Perspective** (Advanced) | 0 to 1 | Lower values flatten the projection; higher values exaggerate depth. |

The background, facecam bubble, canvas annotations, and captions stay upright while you tilt. Only the screen surface itself and its mockup frame move in 3D.

## Wrap the media in a mockup

The **Mockup** section places the tilted surface inside a device or window frame:

- **None** — no mockup.
- **Browser** — a browser chrome with an address bar.
- **Desktop Window** — a macOS-style window with traffic-light buttons.
- **Phone** — a portrait phone bezel.
- **Tablet** — a tablet bezel.

For any mockup other than **None**, pick a **Light** or **Dark** style, and toggle **Edge highlight** to add a subtle rim light around the frame.

## Animate on export

The **Motion** section adds one motion interval to the current edit. Turn on **Animate** to reveal the motion controls.

Motion is intentionally a single interval with a start pose and an end pose. Before the start time the surface holds the start pose; after the end time it holds the end pose. There is no way to chain multiple keyframes on the same clip.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick a motion preset (optional)">
    Open **Motion preset** and choose **Push In**, **Pull Out**, **Tilt Reveal**, or **Side Sweep**. Each preset fills in a matching start pose and end pose that you can then tweak.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the interval">
    Drag the two handles on the range slider, or use the **Start** and **End** number sliders, to place the motion inside the clip. The minimum interval is 0.05s.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set an image duration (image editor only)">
    For animated image exports, set **Duration** (0.25s to 60s) to control how long the exported clip lasts. The interval is clamped to fit inside this duration.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit the poses">
    Use the **Edit pose** segmented control to switch between **Start** and **End**, then adjust the sliders in **Transform**. The preview seeks to the endpoint you are editing so you always see what you are shaping.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick an easing curve">
    Choose **Linear**, **Ease In**, **Ease Out**, or **Ease In Out** to control how the surface accelerates between the two poses.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Turning **Animate** off returns Scene to a single static pose.

## Preview and export

Scene renders the same geometry in the editor preview and on export, so what you see is what you get. When Scene is active, the video editor switches to a Metal-backed preview to keep tilt, motion, and mockup responsive at playback speed.

### Screenshot exports

- **Save PNG…** and **Copy PNG** capture the pose currently displayed in the preview. If motion is on and you have scrubbed to a specific time, the PNG is exported at that time.
- **Export animated image** (from the screenshot editor's export menu) renders the motion interval as a video file. Pick **MP4**, **MOV**, or **GIF** in the export dialog. GIF exports respect the **Loop** toggle.

### Video exports

- Scene is included automatically in any video export. The motion interval is clamped to the exported segment.
- Exported videos use the same **MP4**, **MOV**, and **GIF** formats as any other video export.

## Undo, redo, and reset

- Scene edits use a dedicated undo history while the Scene inspector is active, so undoing a tilt or motion change does not disturb your timeline edits.
- Each section header has its own reset button: **Transform** returns the pose to identity, **Mockup** clears the frame and edge highlight, and **Motion** resets the interval and disables **Animate**.
- **Reset Scene** at the top of the inspector clears every Scene control at once.
