Open Recorder captures from three kinds of sources: screens, windows, and areas. All three live in the unified **Choose Source** picker. Open it by clicking the source chip in the recording HUD, or by starting any capture that needs a source.

## The Choose Source picker

The picker shows a **Choose what to capture** grid with three tabs:

- **Screens**: every connected display, with a thumbnail for each.
- **Windows**: open app windows you can capture individually.
- **Area**: a free-form region you drag out on any display.

Selecting a source stores it on the HUD source chip. Open Recorder remembers your setup for the next capture.

### Screens

The **Screens** tab lists all connected displays. With a single display, the picker shows one large preview. With more than two displays, the grid scrolls so every screen stays reachable.

Entire-screen captures started from shortcuts also route through this tab, so you always confirm which display gets captured before the recording or screenshot begins.

### Windows

The **Windows** tab lists open windows with their app name and title. Pick one to capture just that window.

### Area capture

The **Area** tab starts an interactive drag selection. Open Recorder dims every connected display and shows a **Drag to select an area** overlay on each one.

- Drag on any display to define the capture region. Area selection is not bound to a single screen.
- A badge below the selection shows the live size in pixels, for example `1280 × 720 px`.
- Release the mouse to set the area. In screenshot mode, Open Recorder captures immediately. In recording mode started from a drag-record shortcut, recording starts as soon as you release.
- Press <kbd>Esc</kbd> on any display to cancel the selection.

The selection overlay displays over full-screen apps and follows Space changes, so you can select an area inside a full-screen app. If you connect or disconnect a display while selecting, Open Recorder rebuilds the overlay to match the new display layout.
