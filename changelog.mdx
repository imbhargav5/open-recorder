<Update label="September 10, 2026" description="v0.2.52" tags={["macOS"]}>
## Bug fixes

### First click starts area selection

Dragging an area now works on the first click, even when Open Recorder is not the active app or the overlay is on a secondary display. Previously, the first mouse-down could only focus the selection overlay, so you had to click again before the drag registered. See [Capture sources](/recording/capture-sources#area-capture) for how area selection works.
</Update>

<Update label="September 9, 2026" description="v0.2.51" tags={["macOS"]}>
## Improvements

### Quiet launch

Opening Open Recorder no longer shows the recording HUD. The HUD stays hidden until you choose **Show Recorder** from the menu bar dropdown, start a new capture with **New Recording** or **New Screenshot**, or press a global capture shortcut. Open Recorder still restores your last capture source in the background, so the HUD opens with your previous setup. Onboarding still appears on first run. See [Screen recording](/recording/screen-recording#the-recording-hud) for the full list of HUD behaviors.
</Update>

<Update label="September 8, 2026" description="v0.2.50" tags={["macOS"]}>
## New features

### Signed nightly builds

You can now try upcoming changes early with nightly builds. Each nightly is a signed and notarized app named **OpenRecorderNightly**, built on demand from the repository's **Build Nightly macOS App** GitHub Actions workflow, with downloads for both Apple Silicon and Intel kept for 30 days. Nightly installs alongside the production app with its own preferences, privacy permissions, and capture folders, so testing a nightly never touches your existing setup. Nightly builds do not update automatically.

## Improvements

### Faster styled exports

Exports that use rounded corners, a facecam, a wallpaper, or a blurred background now finish roughly 20 to 30 percent faster. Open Recorder builds the rounded masks for the recording and facecam once per export and reuses them for every frame instead of recreating them frame by frame. Longer recordings benefit most: in testing, a ten-minute 1080p styled export dropped from about 100 seconds to 64 seconds. Exported video quality is unchanged. See [Backgrounds](/editing/backgrounds) for the styling options.

### Smaller, optimized app package

Open Recorder is now packaged with release optimizations enabled, which shrinks the download by about 5 MB.
</Update>

<Update label="September 7, 2026" description="v0.2.49" tags={["macOS"]}>
## New features

### Quit Open Recorder from the HUD

Right-click anywhere on the recording HUD and choose **Quit Open Recorder** to exit the app. The menu item is available whether the HUD is idle or a recording is in progress, so you no longer need to switch to the menu bar to quit. See [Screen recording](/recording/screen-recording) for the full list of HUD interactions.

## Improvements

### Faster styled video exports

Exports that apply a static background, such as a wallpaper, gradient, or solid color, now render noticeably faster. Open Recorder renders the background once and reuses it for every frame instead of recomposing it frame by frame. Exports with video backgrounds and unstyled exports are unaffected.
</Update>

<Update label="September 7, 2026" description="v0.2.48" tags={["macOS"]}>
## New features

### Adaptive automatic zoom

Automatic zoom now plans a smooth camera path across each interaction instead of zooming on isolated clicks. Related actions stay in view together, pans ease between nearby targets, and low-value moments no longer trigger a zoom. A new **Maximum zoom** slider in **Settings** limits generated magnification from 1× to 3× (default 2×). Setting it to 1× disables generated zooms entirely.

The timeline's **Regenerate automatic zooms** button re-analyzes the recording while preserving your manual zoom regions and any automatic regions you have edited. The inspector labels those regions **Edited · protected**. Regeneration applies as a single undoable edit. See [Zoom effects](/editing/zoom-effects) for the full guide.

### Delete all zooms at once

The zoom inspector's **Delete** button now includes a dropdown with a **Delete All Zoom Levels** option, so you can clear every zoom region from the timeline in one action instead of deleting them one by one. See [Zoom effects](/editing/zoom-effects#delete-zoom-regions) for details.

## Bug fixes

### Readable release notes in the in-app updater

The update window now shows formatted release notes grouped into New, Improved, and Fixed sections. Previously, it displayed raw commit text that was hard to read.
</Update>

<Update label="September 3, 2026" description="v0.2.47" tags={["macOS"]}>
## New features

### Record any key combination as a global shortcut

Global shortcuts are no longer limited to preset combinations. Click a shortcut field on the **Shortcuts** tab in **Settings**, then press the keys you want. The field previews modifiers as you hold them, saves the combination when you release the keys, and <kbd>Esc</kbd> cancels recording. Open Recorder validates the combination and shows an error under the field if it conflicts with another action, a menu command, or a shortcut reserved by macOS or registered by another application.

Letter, number, and symbol keys require <kbd>⌘</kbd>, <kbd>⌃</kbd>, or <kbd>⌥</kbd>, while function keys such as <kbd>F5</kbd> can be assigned on their own. **Restore Default Shortcuts** resets every action to its default combination. See [Keyboard shortcuts](/reference/keyboard-shortcuts) for the defaults and full validation rules.
</Update>

<Update label="September 3, 2026" description="v0.2.46" tags={["macOS"]}>
## Updates

### Tabbed Settings window

Settings is now organized into three tabs in a wider window: **General** for recording and folder options, **Shortcuts** for the global capture shortcuts, and **System** for macOS permissions. See [Keyboard shortcuts](/reference/keyboard-shortcuts) for what lives on the Shortcuts tab.

Previously, all settings appeared in a single scrolling list.
</Update>

<Update label="September 2, 2026" description="v0.2.45" tags={["macOS"]}>
## New features

### Open Settings from the menu bar

The menu bar dropdown now includes a **Settings…** item that opens the Settings window. You can also press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>,</kbd> while an Open Recorder window is focused.

## Bug fixes

### No more stacked area selection overlays

Choosing the **Area** tab while an area selection overlay is already open no longer stacks a second overlay. Open Recorder now brings the existing overlay to the front instead. See [Capture sources](/recording/capture-sources) for how area selection works.
</Update>

<Update label="September 1, 2026" description="v0.2.44" tags={["macOS"]}>
## Bug fixes

### Escape cancels the recording countdown

Pressing <kbd>Esc</kbd> during the recording countdown now cancels the recording before capture begins. The countdown overlay closes and the HUD returns to its ready state, so you can adjust the setup and start again. Previously, <kbd>Esc</kbd> only canceled area selection, and the countdown always ran to completion. See [Screen recording](/recording/screen-recording) for countdown behavior.

### Dragged area captures match the selection outline

Screenshots and recordings of a dragged area now capture exactly the region inside the selection outline. Previously, the captured area could be offset from the visible selection by a pixel, most noticeably with odd-sized selections. Recording dimensions are still rounded to even values as required by the video encoder.
</Update>

<Update label="August 28, 2026" description="v0.2.43" tags={["macOS"]}>
## New features

### Auto-zoom follows cursor activity in every preset

Auto-zoom now reacts to cursor activity, such as hovering over a menu or dropdown, in every zoom preset instead of only the Guided preset. Each preset applies its own confidence threshold, so subtler presets still filter out low-confidence moments.

### 12 new photo wallpapers

The **Image** background tab adds 12 new wallpapers, including aurora, ocean waves, starry night, autumn forest, desert dunes, tropical beach, snowy peaks, and lavender field. See [Backgrounds](/editing/backgrounds) for how to apply them.

### More gradients and solid colors

The background picker adds 12 new gradients and 6 new solid colors, covering families like gold, wine, steel blue, espresso, and aurora teal.

## Bug fixes

### Video-loop backgrounds now animate

The **Video** background tab's loops (Aurora, Particles, Cyber Neon, and Gradient Mesh) previously rendered as still images. Each loop now plays a real animated video.

### Recording HUD stays visible while recording

The recording HUD, with the Stop button and timer, no longer disappears when the countdown starts. Previously it could stay hidden for the entire recording, leaving the <kbd>⌘R</kbd> hotkey as the only way to stop. The HUD also no longer reappears on top of the editor after you finish a recording. See [Screen recording](/recording/screen-recording) for HUD behavior.
</Update>

<Update label="August 27, 2026" description="v0.2.42" tags={["macOS"]}>
## Bug fixes

### Capture overlays stay above full-screen apps

Additional fixes keep the recording HUD and countdown overlay on top of full-screen apps, resolving remaining cases where they could still drop behind a full-screen Space.
</Update>

<Update label="August 27, 2026" description="v0.2.41" tags={["macOS"]}>
## Bug fixes

### HUD and countdown overlay over full-screen apps

The recording HUD and the countdown overlay now reliably appear on top of other apps' full-screen Spaces. Previously, either could stay hidden behind a full-screen app, leaving you without recording controls or a visible countdown. See [Screen recording](/recording/screen-recording) for how the HUD behaves during capture.

### Camera stays off when the HUD toggle is off

The camera no longer turns on during a recording when the HUD camera toggle is off. The camera bubble now only arms itself when you have enabled the camera in the HUD.

### Facecam start reliability

Fixed a race between camera prewarm and recording start that could break the facecam session, and stopped leaking the camera bubble and capture session when facecam recording failed to start.
</Update>

<Update label="August 26, 2026" description="v0.2.40" tags={["macOS"]}>
## New features

### Recording HUD over full-screen apps

The recording HUD now displays on top of full-screen macOS apps and follows you across Spaces. You can start, control, and stop recordings without leaving a full-screen app. Learn more in [Screen recording](/recording/screen-recording).

### Multi-monitor area selection

Area capture now works across every connected display. When you choose the **Area** tab in the **Choose Source** picker, Open Recorder shows a drag overlay on all displays at once. Drag on any screen to define the capture region, and press <kbd>Esc</kbd> to cancel. A live badge shows the selection size in pixels. See [Capture sources](/recording/capture-sources) for details.
</Update>
