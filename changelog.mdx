<Update label="August 28, 2026" description="v0.2.43" tags={["macOS"]}>
## New features

### Auto-zoom follows cursor activity in every preset

Auto-zoom now reacts to cursor activity, such as hovering over a menu or dropdown, in every zoom preset instead of only the Guided preset. Each preset applies its own confidence threshold, so subtler presets still filter out low-confidence moments.

### 12 new photo wallpapers

The **Image** background tab adds 12 new wallpapers, including aurora, ocean waves, starry night, autumn forest, desert dunes, tropical beach, snowy peaks, and lavender field. See [Backgrounds](/editing/backgrounds) for how to apply them.

### More gradients and solid colors

The background picker adds 12 new gradients and 6 new solid colors, covering families like gold, wine, steel blue, espresso, and aurora teal.

## Bug fixes

### Video-loop backgrounds now animate

The **Video** background tab's loops (Aurora, Particles, Cyber Neon, and Gradient Mesh) previously rendered as still images. Each loop now plays a real animated video.

### Recording HUD stays visible while recording

The recording HUD, with the Stop button and timer, no longer disappears when the countdown starts. Previously it could stay hidden for the entire recording, leaving the <kbd>⌘R</kbd> hotkey as the only way to stop. The HUD also no longer reappears on top of the editor after you finish a recording. See [Screen recording](/recording/screen-recording) for HUD behavior.
</Update>

<Update label="August 27, 2026" description="v0.2.42" tags={["macOS"]}>
## Bug fixes

### Capture overlays stay above full-screen apps

Additional fixes keep the recording HUD and countdown overlay on top of full-screen apps, resolving remaining cases where they could still drop behind a full-screen Space.
</Update>

<Update label="August 27, 2026" description="v0.2.41" tags={["macOS"]}>
## Bug fixes

### HUD and countdown overlay over full-screen apps

The recording HUD and the countdown overlay now reliably appear on top of other apps' full-screen Spaces. Previously, either could stay hidden behind a full-screen app, leaving you without recording controls or a visible countdown. See [Screen recording](/recording/screen-recording) for how the HUD behaves during capture.

### Camera stays off when the HUD toggle is off

The camera no longer turns on during a recording when the HUD camera toggle is off. The camera bubble now only arms itself when you have enabled the camera in the HUD.

### Facecam start reliability

Fixed a race between camera prewarm and recording start that could break the facecam session, and stopped leaking the camera bubble and capture session when facecam recording failed to start.
</Update>

<Update label="August 26, 2026" description="v0.2.40" tags={["macOS"]}>
## New features

### Recording HUD over full-screen apps

The recording HUD now displays on top of full-screen macOS apps and follows you across Spaces. You can start, control, and stop recordings without leaving a full-screen app. Learn more in [Screen recording](/recording/screen-recording).

### Multi-monitor area selection

Area capture now works across every connected display. When you choose the **Area** tab in the **Choose Source** picker, Open Recorder shows a drag overlay on all displays at once. Drag on any screen to define the capture region, and press <kbd>Esc</kbd> to cancel. A live badge shows the selection size in pixels. See [Capture sources](/recording/capture-sources) for details.
</Update>
