<Update label="August 26, 2026" description="v0.2.40" tags={["macOS"]}>
## New features

### Recording HUD over full-screen apps

The recording HUD now displays on top of full-screen macOS apps and follows you across Spaces. You can start, control, and stop recordings without leaving a full-screen app. Learn more in [Screen recording](/recording/screen-recording).

### Multi-monitor area selection

Area capture now works across every connected display. When you choose the **Area** tab in the **Choose Source** picker, Open Recorder shows a drag overlay on all displays at once. Drag on any screen to define the capture region, and press <kbd>Esc</kbd> to cancel. A live badge shows the selection size in pixels. See [Capture sources](/recording/capture-sources) for details.
</Update>
