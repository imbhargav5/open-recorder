<Update label="August 27, 2026" description="v0.2.41" tags={["macOS"]}>
## Bug fixes

### HUD and countdown overlay over full-screen apps

The recording HUD and the countdown overlay now reliably appear on top of other apps' full-screen Spaces. Previously, either could stay hidden behind a full-screen app, leaving you without recording controls or a visible countdown. See [Screen recording](/recording/screen-recording) for how the HUD behaves during capture.

### Camera stays off when the HUD toggle is off

The camera no longer turns on during a recording when the HUD camera toggle is off. The camera bubble now only arms itself when you have enabled the camera in the HUD.

### Facecam start reliability

Fixed a race between camera prewarm and recording start that could break the facecam session, and stopped leaking the camera bubble and capture session when facecam recording failed to start.
</Update>

<Update label="August 26, 2026" description="v0.2.40" tags={["macOS"]}>
## New features

### Recording HUD over full-screen apps

The recording HUD now displays on top of full-screen macOS apps and follows you across Spaces. You can start, control, and stop recordings without leaving a full-screen app. Learn more in [Screen recording](/recording/screen-recording).

### Multi-monitor area selection

Area capture now works across every connected display. When you choose the **Area** tab in the **Choose Source** picker, Open Recorder shows a drag overlay on all displays at once. Drag on any screen to define the capture region, and press <kbd>Esc</kbd> to cancel. A live badge shows the selection size in pixels. See [Capture sources](/recording/capture-sources) for details.
</Update>
